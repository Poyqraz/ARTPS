\subsection{Supplementary Human-Reviewed Evaluation}
Table~\ref{tab:indep-v11} reports a validation-only audit under
\texttt{independent\_eval\_v1\_1}.
Frozen ARTPS image scores from \texttt{artps\_full\_frozen\_mars\_clf\_on\_v1} (FP32) are
\texttt{max\_valid\_candidate\_after\_masks}; labels were remapped with no tuning and no
inference rerun.
Curiosity ranking, diversity penalty, and the Priority Buffer were not applied on these
scores.
This subsection is explicitly distinct from Table~\ref{tab:accepted-abstract}.
AUROC is the principal ranking-oriented metric for this supplementary validation.

The predeclared F1-based validation threshold selection collapsed to an
all-positive operating point.
Consequently, threshold-dependent classification metrics are not treated as
performance measures in this supplementary evaluation; AUROC is used as the
principal ranking-oriented metric.
