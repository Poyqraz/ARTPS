\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

\subsection{ARTPS Performance}
ARTPS achieved an AUROC of $0.894$, an AUPRC of $0.847$, and an F1 score of $0.823$ on NASA
Mars rover imagery.
The PaDiM/PatchCore baseline achieved an AUROC of $0.856$.
In the lightweight configuration, the preprocessing--fusion--localization core achieved
$28.1$\,FPS at $256\times256$ on the development workstation
(Table~\ref{tab:accepted-abstract}).

\begin{table}[t]

\subsection{Human-Reviewed Validation}
On the 54-image validation partition ($46$ positive, $8$ negative), the fixed image-level
ARTPS score yielded AUROC~$0.772$ and AP~$0.956$
(Table~\ref{tab:indep-v11}).
Curiosity ranking, diversity penalty, and the Priority Buffer were not applied on these
scores.
AUROC is the principal ranking-oriented metric for this analysis.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering

\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lc@{}}

Reviewed / unchanged / relabeled & $360$ / $188$ / $172$ \\
Initial heuristic labels (pos./neg.) & $180$ / $180$ \\
Human-reviewed labels (pos./neg.) & $340$ / $20$ \\
\midrule
\multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\emph{Validation subset ($46$ pos.\ / $8$ neg.)}} \\

\end{table}

The predeclared F1-based validation threshold selection collapsed to an
all-positive operating point.
Consequently, threshold-dependent classification metrics are not treated as
performance measures in this analysis; AUROC is used as the
principal ranking-oriented metric.

\subsection{Qualitative processing example}
Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} is a single non-test illustration of RGB input, the
post-suppression combined anomaly map, relative depth, and candidate-support overlay under the
ARTPS image-level scoring path.
Fig.~\ref{fig:cue-decomp} decomposes that same fusion path into reconstruction,
relative-depth-edge, and texture cues plus the pre-suppression fused map; it is not a new
detection metric.
In addition to that single-scene example and cue decomposition, Fig.~\ref{fig:close-far}
shows one close-range and one distant-scene illustration from preselected non-test scenes,
to show how the ARTPS pipeline behaves under different apparent object-scale
contexts.
These examples are qualitative and are not a controlled near/far benchmark.
