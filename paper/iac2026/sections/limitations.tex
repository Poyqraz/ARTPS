\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}
The ARTPS depth representation provides relative near/far ordering.
Metric range, physical dimensions, and calibrated 3-D reconstruction require calibrated
geometry~\citep{ranftl2021dpt,ranftl2022robust}.

Figures~\ref{fig:qualitative}--\ref{fig:close-far} provide qualitative mechanism illustrations;
their evidentiary role is separate from the quantitative evaluation.
Human-reviewed performance is based on the 54-image validation partition; the paired
54-image test partition is reserved for future evaluation.
The labels represent repeat-author review.
The human-reviewed set contains $20$ negatives among $360$ images ($8$ validation,
$2$ test).
The present human-reviewed validation subset contains eight negative
examples, making operating-threshold calibration unstable and limiting the precision of
the AUROC estimate.
The image-level score terminates at Layer~B; curiosity, diversity, and Priority Buffer
operate in Layer~C.
Checkpoint training-set lineage requires independent cross-checking against the
human-reviewed corpus; accordingly, this evidence is categorized as internal validation.
The current hardware evidence covers lightweight workstation timing; full-pipeline Jetson
timing remains a future hardware-validation stage.

Current evidence covers a safety-aware, flight-readiness-oriented software architecture
within a defined operational envelope.
Flight qualification, mission certification, and system-level safety assurance require
independent mission-specific V\&V.
