\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ARTPS combines multi-cue anomaly detection, monocular relative depth, operational suppression,
apparent size--distance policy, diversity-aware ranking, and a Priority Buffer for onboard
target prioritization.
The reported evaluation uses the fixed-coefficient Layer~B image-score path rather than
entropy-weighted fusion.
ARTPS achieved AUROC~$0.894$, AUPRC~$0.847$, and F1~$0.823$ in the primary Mars-rover
evaluation.
The additional human-reviewed validation yielded AUROC~$0.772$ under the fixed image-level
scoring configuration.
Qualitative analyses illustrate the contributions of cue fusion, suppression, and localization
across representative scene scales.

Future work includes a harder-negative or independently annotated challenge set,
full-pipeline edge-hardware timing, and mission-specific V\&V.
