\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ARTPS specifies an onboard target-prioritization architecture that couples multi-cue visual
anomaly responses with monocular relative depth, an entropy-weighted fusion specification,
operational suppression, apparent size--distance policy, diversity ranking, and a Priority
Buffer.
The current supplementary frozen evaluation uses the fixed-coefficient Layer~B image-score
path rather than entropy-weighted fusion.

Table~\ref{tab:eval-tracks} summarizes the evaluation tracks: historical accepted-abstract
numbers are retained, and supplementary validation reports AUROC~$0.772$ under frozen
fixed-coefficient scores without opening the held-out test.
A qualitative non-test processing example illustrates the frozen image-score path without
adding a new benchmark.

Future work includes a harder-negative or independently annotated challenge set, archival
reconstruction of the historical experiment, full-pipeline edge-hardware timing, and
mission-specific V\&V.
