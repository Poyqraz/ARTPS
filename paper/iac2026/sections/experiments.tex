\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}
This section describes the two evaluation components used in this study
(Table~\ref{tab:eval-tracks}).
A qualitative processing example (Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative}) illustrates the image-level
scoring path only; it is not a third quantitative evaluation.

\subsection{Primary performance evaluation}
ARTPS was evaluated for detection and lightweight runtime performance on NASA Mars rover
imagery.
The study reports AUROC~$0.894$, AUPRC~$0.847$, F1~$0.823$, a PaDiM/PatchCore baseline
AUROC~$0.856$, and $28.1$\,FPS at $256\times256$ for a lightweight preprocessing--fusion--localization
core.
Table~\ref{tab:accepted-abstract} restates those values; lightweight FPS is reported only
there.

\subsection{Human-reviewed validation protocol}
A homogeneous Curiosity Mastcam-oriented corpus of $360$ images was assembled with fixed split
assignments of $252$ train / $54$ validation / $54$ test.
All $360$ images received a model-blind repeat-author review under a written science-interest
annotation guide.
After review the corpus contains $340$ positive and $20$ negative images; the validation
partition contains $46$ positive and $8$ negative images.
This is not an independent second annotation.
The 54-image test partition was reserved and was not used for ARTPS inference, threshold
selection, or the human-reviewed performance results reported in this study.
Numeric validation results use the reviewed labels with no additional tuning and without
repeating inference.
Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} shows a qualitative non-test Mars scene selected before inference
using predefined scene-composition criteria, under the image-level scoring path.
Fig.~\ref{fig:close-far} adds one close-range and one distant-scene illustration from
preselected non-test scenes; it is not a range-conditioned performance study.

\subsection{Dataset and annotation semantics}
One homogeneous Curiosity Mastcam-oriented image domain was selected from inventory metadata
without model-score inspection.
The human-reviewed corpus comprises $360$ included images with fixed split assignments of
$252$ train / $54$ validation / $54$ test.
All $360$ images received a repeat-author model-blind review; this is not independent double
annotation.
A positive label denotes at least one real surface object or region that is visually distinct
from its surroundings and scientifically worth follow-up under the annotation guide.
A negative label denotes no distinct science-interest target under that guide.
Rover hardware, border artefacts, telemetry overlays, compression or noise artefacts, a
shadow boundary alone, or an exposure/specular artefact alone do not count as positive.

\subsection{Baselines and metrics}
PaDiM/PatchCore is the anomaly-detection baseline~\citep{defard2020padim,roth2022patchcore}.
PaDiM and PatchCore remain separate baselines and are not active components of the reported
image-level score.
Primary evaluation metrics are AUROC, AUPRC, F1, the baseline AUROC, and lightweight-core FPS.
For the human-reviewed validation, AUROC is the principal ranking-oriented metric and AP is a
secondary metric reported with prevalence context.
The human-reviewed validation threshold produced an all-positive operating point; therefore
threshold-dependent F1 is not interpreted as a performance measure on that analysis.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
The evaluation uses a fixed configuration, checkpoint hashes, and the image-score definition
stated above.
There was no test inference, no test metric, and no threshold retuning on held-out images.
Runs are aborted when required inputs or predefined evaluation conditions are incomplete.

\subsection{Hardware scope}
The lightweight preprocessing--fusion--localization configuration achieved $28.1$\,FPS at
$256\times256$ on the development workstation.
This measurement excludes learned DPT and autoencoder inference.
The present study does not report full-pipeline edge-hardware or Jetson timing.
No flight qualification is claimed.
