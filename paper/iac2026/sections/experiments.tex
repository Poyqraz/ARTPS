\section{Experimental Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}
The quantitative study comprises the two evaluation components summarized in
Table~\ref{tab:eval-tracks}; Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} provides qualitative process
visualization.

\subsection{Primary performance evaluation}
ARTPS was evaluated for detection and lightweight runtime performance on a $2{,}847$-image
Mars-rover corpus including $1{,}247$ Curiosity Mastcam images~\citep{malin2017mastcam}
(Sol 100--1700) and
$892$ Perseverance Mastcam-Z images~\citep{bell2021mastcamz} (Sol 1--400).
Within this corpus, a $708$-image test/validation evaluation subset was used under diverse
field conditions.
Imagery spans approximately $640\times480$ to $1920\times1080$.
The evaluation used identical train/validation/test splits and consistent random seeds.
Decision thresholds were selected on validation data and the reported primary performance
was measured on the test set.
The PaDiM/PatchCore baseline entry used WRN-50-2 features under the same partition and
preprocessing policy as ARTPS and achieved AUROC~$0.856$.
The study reports ARTPS AUROC~$0.894$, AUPRC~$0.847$, F1~$0.823$, and $28.1$\,FPS at
$256\times256$ for a lightweight preprocessing--fusion--localization core.
Table~\ref{tab:accepted-abstract} summarizes detection and lightweight runtime performance.

\subsection{Human-reviewed validation protocol}
A homogeneous Curiosity Mastcam-oriented corpus of $360$ images was assembled with fixed split
assignments of $252$ train / $54$ validation / $54$ test.
All $360$ images received a model-blind repeat-author review under a written science-interest
annotation guide.
After review the corpus contains $340$ positive and $20$ negative images; the validation
partition contains $46$ positive and $8$ negative images.
Human-reviewed performance is reported on the 54-image validation partition; the corresponding
54-image test partition remains reserved.
Numeric validation results apply the reviewed labels directly to the precomputed inference
scores under the fixed evaluation configuration.
Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} shows a qualitative non-test Mars scene selected before inference
using predefined scene-composition criteria, under the image-level scoring path.
Fig.~\ref{fig:close-far} provides qualitative apparent-scale context across close and distant
scenes; range-conditioned performance remains outside this analysis.

\subsection{Dataset and annotation semantics}
One homogeneous Curiosity Mastcam-oriented image domain was selected from inventory metadata
without model-score inspection.
The human-reviewed corpus comprises $360$ included images with fixed split assignments of
$252$ train / $54$ validation / $54$ test.
The labels represent repeat-author model-blind review.
A positive label denotes at least one real surface object or region that is visually distinct
from its surroundings and scientifically worth follow-up under the annotation guide.
A negative label denotes no distinct science-interest target under that guide.
Positive inclusion excludes rover hardware, border artefacts, telemetry overlays, compression
or noise artefacts, a shadow boundary alone, and an exposure/specular artefact alone.

\subsection{Baselines and metrics}
PaDiM/PatchCore is the anomaly-detection baseline~\citep{defard2020padim,roth2022patchcore},
with a WRN-50-2 backbone under the same partition and preprocessing policy as the primary
evaluation.
The human-reviewed Layer~B configuration sets the PaDiM/PatchCore score terms to zero;
baseline performance is evaluated separately.
Primary evaluation metrics are AUROC, AUPRC, F1, the baseline AUROC, and lightweight-core FPS.
For the human-reviewed validation, AUROC is the principal ranking-oriented metric and AP is a
secondary metric reported with prevalence context.
The all-positive operating point renders threshold-dependent F1 uninformative for this
validation; AUROC and AP therefore characterize ranking performance.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
For the human-reviewed validation, the inference configuration, checkpoint hashes,
preprocessing profile, and image-score definition were fixed.
Runs are aborted when required inputs or predefined evaluation conditions are incomplete.

\subsection{Hardware scope}
The $28.1$\,FPS measurement characterizes the lightweight preprocessing--fusion--localization
core at $256\times256$ on the development workstation, excluding learned DPT and autoencoder
inference.
Full-pipeline edge-hardware timing and flight qualification constitute separate hardware and
mission V\&V stages.
